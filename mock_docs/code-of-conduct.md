# Code of Conduct and Workplace Behaviour Policy
**Apex Solutions — People & Culture**  
**Version 1.8 | Effective January 2025**

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out the standards of behaviour expected of all Apex Solutions employees, contractors, and representatives. It applies in all work settings — offices, client sites, remote work environments, and company events.

Apex Solutions is committed to a workplace that is respectful, inclusive, and safe for everyone. This policy exists not to constrain but to establish a shared baseline that allows diverse people to work together effectively.

---

## 2. Core Principles

All employees are expected to act in accordance with the following principles:

**Integrity:** Be honest and transparent in your dealings with colleagues, clients, and the company. Do not misrepresent your work, qualifications, or the company's products and services.

**Respect:** Treat every person with dignity regardless of their role, background, or seniority. Disagreement is healthy; disrespect is not.

**Accountability:** Take ownership of your work, your mistakes, and their consequences. Do not deflect or minimise when things go wrong.

**Confidentiality:** Protect the company's confidential information and that of its clients. Do not share sensitive information with unauthorised parties, including family members.

**Professionalism:** Represent Apex Solutions positively in all interactions. This includes communications on social media where you are identifiable as an employee.

---

## 3. Prohibited Conduct

The following behaviours are prohibited and may result in disciplinary action up to and including dismissal:

### 3.1 Harassment and Discrimination
- Discrimination on the basis of race, gender, age, religion, disability, sexual orientation, national origin, or any other protected characteristic
- Sexual harassment, including unwanted physical contact, inappropriate comments, or sending offensive material
- Bullying: repeated unreasonable behaviour directed at an employee that creates a risk to their health and safety
- Creating or contributing to a hostile work environment

### 3.2 Dishonesty and Fraud
- Falsifying timesheets, expense claims, or any company records
- Misappropriation of company funds, equipment, or other assets
- Providing false information during recruitment or at any point during employment
- Plagiarism or misrepresentation of work

### 3.3 Conflicts of Interest
- Engaging in external work or business activities that compete with Apex Solutions without prior written disclosure and approval
- Using company resources, contacts, or information for personal financial gain
- Participating in a decision that directly benefits a family member or close associate without disclosing the relationship

### 3.4 Workplace Safety
- Reporting to work under the influence of alcohol or illegal substances
- Engaging in behaviour that endangers the physical safety of colleagues
- Ignoring safety procedures or emergency protocols

---

## 4. Communication Standards

### Internal Communications
All written communications (email, Slack, documents) should be professional and respectful. Assume that any written communication could be seen by anyone in the company.

Avoid: all-caps messages, passive-aggressive language, copying managers on routine messages as a form of pressure, and sending messages outside of business hours with an implicit expectation of immediate response (use Slack's scheduled send feature instead).

### External Communications
Employees communicating with clients or partners represent the company. Communications should be clear, measured, and consistent with agreed positions. Do not make commitments to clients that have not been approved by your manager.

### Social Media
Employees may identify themselves as working at Apex Solutions on personal social media. When doing so:
- Do not share confidential client or company information
- Do not make statements that could be interpreted as official company positions
- Do not disparage clients, colleagues, or the company
- Add a disclaimer such as "views are my own" where appropriate

---

## 5. Reporting Concerns

Employees who witness or experience behaviour that violates this policy are encouraged to report it. Reports can be made to:

- **Direct manager** (if the concern does not involve the manager)
- **People & Culture:** hr@apexsolutions.internal
- **Anonymous reporting line:** ethics@apexsolutions.internal (reviewed by an independent party)

All reports are treated confidentially to the extent possible. Retaliation against an employee for making a good-faith report is itself a violation of this policy and will be dealt with seriously.

---

## 6. Disciplinary Process

Violations of this policy are handled through Apex Solutions' standard disciplinary process:

1. **Informal discussion** — for minor first-time issues, the manager addresses the behaviour directly with the employee
2. **Formal written warning** — issued for more serious violations or repeated minor violations
3. **Final written warning** — issued when prior warnings have not resulted in improvement
4. **Dismissal** — for gross misconduct or failure to improve after warnings

Certain behaviours (fraud, sexual harassment, serious safety violations) may result in immediate dismissal without prior warning.

Employees subject to formal disciplinary action have the right to be accompanied by a colleague or union representative during meetings.

---

## 7. New Employees

All new employees are required to complete the following during their first week:
- Read and acknowledge this Code of Conduct
- Complete the online harassment prevention module (available on the learning portal)
- Attend the orientation session covering company values and workplace expectations

Acknowledgement is recorded in the HR portal. Failure to complete these items within the first 10 working days will be flagged to the employee's manager.
