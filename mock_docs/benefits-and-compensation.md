# Employee Benefits and Compensation Guide
**Apex Solutions — People & Culture**  
**Version 2.0 | Effective January 2025**

---

## 1. Overview

This guide summarises the benefits available to Apex Solutions employees. Eligibility varies by employment type and tenure as noted in each section. Benefits are reviewed annually and may be updated; employees will be notified of material changes with at least 30 days' notice.

---

## 2. Compensation

### 2.1 Pay Cycles
Employees are paid monthly on the last working day of each month. Payslips are available electronically in the HR portal 3 working days before payment.

### 2.2 Annual Salary Reviews
Salary reviews take place annually in March, effective 1 April. Reviews are based on individual performance ratings from the preceding year's performance cycle and market benchmarking data. A performance rating of "Meets Expectations" or above is required to be eligible for a merit increase.

### 2.3 Performance Bonuses
Employees at Consultant level and above are eligible for an annual performance bonus. Bonus targets are communicated at the start of each financial year and are calculated as a percentage of base salary. Bonus payments are made in April following the close of the financial year.

New employees who joined after 1 October of the relevant financial year receive a pro-rated bonus for their period of employment.

### 2.4 Expense Reimbursement
Business expenses (travel, client entertainment, training materials) are reimbursed upon submission of valid receipts through the expense management platform. Expenses must be submitted within 30 days of being incurred. Claims submitted after 30 days may not be approved.

Pre-approval is required for any single expense exceeding R5,000. Travel bookings must be made through the company's preferred travel platform unless prior approval for an alternative is obtained.

---

## 3. Medical Aid

Apex Solutions contributes to medical aid for all permanent employees. The company covers 60% of the employee's medical aid premium and 50% of premiums for registered dependants.

The company's preferred medical aid scheme is outlined in the benefits guide distributed during onboarding. Employees may elect to join a different registered scheme, but the company contribution will be capped at the equivalent of the preferred scheme's standard option.

Medical aid enrolment must be completed within 30 days of the employee's start date. Enrolment outside this window may require a waiting period as per the scheme's rules.

---

## 4. Retirement Fund

All permanent employees are enrolled in the company provident fund from their first day of employment. Contributions are as follows:

- **Employee contribution:** 5% of monthly basic salary
- **Company contribution:** 8% of monthly basic salary

Employees may increase their own contribution up to a maximum of 15% of basic salary. Contributions above 5% are made via salary sacrifice and have tax implications — employees are encouraged to consult with the fund's financial advisor (available at no cost through the fund portal) before changing contribution levels.

Fund statements are issued quarterly and available in the fund portal. Employees are encouraged to nominate beneficiaries on the fund portal — this is not automatic and must be done actively.

---

## 5. Learning and Development

### 5.1 Learning Budget
All permanent employees receive an annual learning budget of R15,000 for approved training, courses, certifications, and conferences. The budget resets on 1 January and does not carry over.

To use the learning budget:
1. Submit a learning request via the HR portal including course details and cost
2. Obtain manager approval
3. Upon completion, submit proof of completion to HR to have the expense processed

### 5.2 Certification Bonuses
Employees who obtain approved technical certifications receive a one-time bonus:

| Certification Level | Bonus |
|---|---|
| Foundational (e.g. IBM watsonx Essentials) | R2,000 |
| Associate / Professional (e.g. AWS Solutions Architect Associate) | R5,000 |
| Expert / Specialty (e.g. IBM Certified Architect) | R10,000 |

Certification bonuses are paid in the month following submission of the certificate. The list of approved certifications is maintained on the learning portal and updated quarterly.

### 5.3 Study Assistance
Employees pursuing a formal degree or diploma relevant to their role may apply for study assistance covering up to 50% of tuition fees, capped at R30,000 per academic year. Study assistance requires a 12-month retention agreement — if the employee resigns within 12 months of receiving assistance, a pro-rated repayment applies.

---

## 6. Wellness Benefits

### 6.1 Employee Assistance Programme (EAP)
All employees and their immediate family members have access to the EAP, which provides:
- Up to 6 free counselling sessions per year (telephonic or in-person)
- Legal and financial advice helpline
- Crisis support (24/7 telephonic)

The EAP is completely confidential. Usage is not reported to the company. Access details are provided during onboarding.

### 6.2 Gym Subsidy
Employees receive a monthly gym subsidy of R500, reimbursed against a valid gym membership invoice submitted through the expense platform. The subsidy applies to any registered gym facility.

### 6.3 Mental Health Days
In addition to sick leave, employees are entitled to 2 mental health days per calendar year — paid leave that does not require a medical certificate or any justification. These days may not be carried over and are separate from the standard sick leave entitlement.

---

## 7. Flexible Work Arrangements

### 7.1 Hybrid Work
Employees are expected to work from the office at least 3 days per week unless their role is designated as fully remote. Office days are coordinated at team level — managers communicate expected in-office days to their teams.

### 7.2 Flexible Hours
Core hours are 09:00–15:00 on working days. Outside of core hours, employees have flexibility to structure their day to suit personal needs, subject to meeting their responsibilities and being available for scheduled meetings.

### 7.3 Work From Abroad
Employees may request to work from outside the country for up to 4 weeks per calendar year. Requests require manager approval and HR review (tax and visa implications must be assessed for each country). Work from abroad does not change the employee's employment terms or compensation.

---

## 8. Onboarding Benefits

New employees receive the following during their first month:

- **Welcome kit:** company-branded notebook, pen set, and laptop bag delivered on day one
- **Buddy programme:** each new employee is paired with a buddy from their team for their first 90 days
- **Onboarding stipend:** R1,500 once-off payment in the first payroll to cover home office setup costs (remote/hybrid employees only)
