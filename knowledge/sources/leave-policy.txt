# Leave and Time Off Policy
**Apex Solutions — People & Culture**  
**Version 3.1 | Effective January 2025**

---

## 1. Annual Leave

All full-time employees are entitled to 21 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive leave on a pro-rata basis calculated against a standard 40-hour week.

Leave accrues from the employee's start date at a rate of 1.75 days per month. Employees may not take annual leave during their first 30 days of employment.

**Carrying over leave:** A maximum of 5 unused leave days may be carried over into the following calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March or they are forfeited. Requests to carry over more than 5 days require written approval from the employee's direct manager and the Head of People & Culture.

**Leave encashment:** Annual leave may not be encashed except upon resignation or termination, in which case unused accrued leave will be paid out at the employee's daily rate.

---

## 2. Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave does not accrue and resets on 1 January each year.

For absences of 1–2 consecutive days, self-certification is accepted. For absences of 3 or more consecutive days, a medical certificate from a registered practitioner must be submitted to HR within 5 working days of returning to work.

Sick leave may not be used to extend annual leave. If an employee falls ill during a period of approved annual leave and can provide a medical certificate, the days may be converted to sick leave at HR's discretion.

---

## 3. Family Responsibility Leave

Employees are entitled to 3 days of paid family responsibility leave per calendar year for the following events:
- Death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling)
- Serious illness of a dependent child requiring the employee's presence
- Birth of the employee's child (in addition to any parental leave entitlement)

Family responsibility leave does not accrue and cannot be carried over. Supporting documentation (e.g. death certificate, medical letter) must be submitted to HR within 10 working days.

---

## 4. Parental Leave

**Primary caregiver leave:** Employees who are the primary caregiver for a newborn or newly adopted child are entitled to 16 weeks of paid parental leave. This applies regardless of gender.

**Secondary caregiver leave:** Employees who are not the primary caregiver are entitled to 4 weeks of paid parental leave.

Parental leave may commence up to 2 weeks before the expected birth date. Employees must notify HR in writing at least 8 weeks before the intended start of parental leave. Failure to provide notice may result in the start date being deferred.

Parental leave runs concurrently with any statutory entitlements.

---

## 5. Study Leave

Employees pursuing qualifications relevant to their role may apply for up to 5 days of paid study leave per academic year for examination preparation and sitting. Applications must be submitted to the employee's manager at least 4 weeks in advance and are subject to operational requirements.

Study leave is not guaranteed and is approved at management discretion. It may not be used for coursework, lectures, or online learning — only for examinations.

---

## 6. Unpaid Leave

Employees may apply for unpaid leave for personal reasons not covered by other leave categories. Unpaid leave requires approval from both the direct manager and the Head of People & Culture. It is not guaranteed and will be assessed against business needs.

Unpaid leave exceeding 20 consecutive working days will affect annual leave accrual for the period of absence.

---

## 7. Leave Application Process

All leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance (except sick leave and emergency family responsibility leave). Retroactive leave requests are not accepted except in genuine emergencies and require Head of People & Culture approval.

Managers are expected to respond to leave requests within 3 working days. If a request is declined, the manager must provide a written reason.

---

## 8. Public Holidays

Employees are entitled to all gazetted public holidays. Where operational requirements necessitate working on a public holiday, the employee will receive either a day off in lieu (to be taken within 30 days) or a 1.5× pay supplement, at the employee's election.
