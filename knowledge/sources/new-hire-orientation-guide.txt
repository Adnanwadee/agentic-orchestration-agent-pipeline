# New Hire Orientation Guide
**Apex Solutions — Welcome to the Team**  
**People & Culture | 2025 Edition**

---

## Welcome

Welcome to Apex Solutions. This guide covers everything you need to get set up, oriented, and productive in your first two weeks. Read it end to end on your first day — it will answer most of the questions you'll have before you think to ask them.

Your first point of contact for anything not covered here is your HR buddy, assigned in your onboarding email, and your direct manager.

---

## Week 1 Schedule

### Day 1 — Setup and Orientation

| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 09:00 | Welcome meeting with your manager |
| 09:30 | IT setup: collect equipment, confirm system access |
| 10:30 | HR onboarding session: contracts, benefits enrolment, policy acknowledgements |
| 12:00 | Welcome lunch with your immediate team |
| 13:30 | Self-guided: complete online modules (see below) |
| 15:00 | Meet your buddy — 30-minute introductory call |
| 16:00 | Free time: explore the intranet, read team documentation |

### Day 2–3 — Company and Role Context

- Complete the Company Overview module on the learning portal (approx. 2 hours)
- Attend your team's regular stand-up or weekly meeting
- Shadow your buddy or a senior colleague on a current task
- Schedule introductory meetings with key stakeholders (your manager will advise who)

### Day 4–5 — Tools and Access

- Confirm all role-specific system access is working (see IT Access Policy)
- Complete any pending access requests through the IT portal
- Set up your development environment or core tooling (your team will share their setup guide)
- Attend the **Orientation Session** — see Section below

## Mandatory First-Week Completions

The following must be completed within your first 5 working days. Completion is tracked in the HR portal.

- [ ] Sign and return your employment contract (if not already done)
- [ ] Enrol in the medical aid scheme (30-day window from start date)
- [ ] Nominate provident fund beneficiaries in the fund portal
- [ ] Read and acknowledge: Code of Conduct
- [ ] Read and acknowledge: IT Access and Equipment Policy
- [ ] Read and acknowledge: Leave Policy
- [ ] Complete: Harassment Prevention online module (approx. 45 minutes)
- [ ] Complete: Information Security Awareness online module (approx. 30 minutes)
- [ ] Attend: New Hire Orientation Session

Your manager will check in at the end of week one to confirm these are done.

## Orientation Session

**What it is:** A half-day session (09:00–13:00) run by People & Culture covering company history, values, business strategy, and how teams are structured. Includes a Q&A panel with senior leaders.

**When it runs:** Every two weeks on a Wednesday. Your first available session after your start date is pre-booked for you unless you were hired within 3 days of a session date, in which case you attend the following one.

**Where:** Main boardroom, 4th floor (or virtually via Teams for remote employees — a link is sent with your booking confirmation).

**What to bring:** Your questions. The Q&A panel is genuinely open — ask what you want to know about the company.

If you need to change your orientation booking, contact hr@apexsolutions.internal at least 2 working days before the session.

## Office Practicalities

**Location:** 12 Innovation Drive, Sandton. Parking is available in the basement — register your vehicle registration with reception on your first day to receive an access tag.

**Access card:** Issued by reception on day 1. Keep it with you at all times in the office. Lost cards must be reported immediately to reception and IT Security.

**Kitchen and breakrooms:** Fully stocked with coffee, tea, and filtered water. A catered lunch is provided on Tuesdays in the main kitchen. Halal, vegetarian, and vegan options are always available — flag other dietary requirements to HR.

**Dress code:** Smart casual. Client-facing days or external events may require formal dress — your manager will advise.

**Meeting rooms:** Bookable through the Outlook calendar system. Rooms not used within 15 minutes of the booking start time are automatically released.

---

## Remote and Hybrid Working

If your role is hybrid, your expected in-office days will be communicated by your manager in your first week. The company minimum is 3 in-office days per week unless your role is designated fully remote.

For your first two weeks, you are expected to be in the office full-time regardless of your standard arrangement. This is to support your onboarding — meeting people in person in your first fortnight makes a measurable difference to how quickly you settle in.

VPN is required for remote access to all internal systems. The VPN client is pre-installed on your company device. If you have connectivity issues, contact the IT helpdesk.

---

## Getting Help After Your First Week

Once your formal onboarding period ends, the main channels for getting help are:

- **Your manager:** for anything related to work, priorities, or professional development
- **Your team:** Slack channels and team meetings are the fastest way to get unblocked on day-to-day tasks
- **HR portal:** for self-service HR tasks (leave requests, payslips, policy lookups)
- **IT portal:** for any technical issues or access needs
- **People & Culture:** for anything sensitive or that doesn't fit another channel

You also have access to the Employee Assistance Programme for personal support — see the Benefits guide for details. It is confidential and nothing is reported back to the company.
