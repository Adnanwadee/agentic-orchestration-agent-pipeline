# IT Access and Equipment Policy
**Apex Solutions — Information Technology**  
**Version 2.4 | Effective March 2025**

---

## 1. Overview

This policy governs the provisioning, use, and return of IT equipment and system access at Apex Solutions. All employees are required to read and acknowledge this policy before IT access is granted.

IT access requests for new employees must be submitted by the hiring manager at least 3 working days before the employee's start date. Late requests may result in the employee not having full system access on their first day.

---

## 2. Standard Equipment Allocation

All full-time employees receive the following standard equipment upon joining:

| Role Category | Laptop | Monitor | Accessories |
|---|---|---|---|
| Developer / Engineer | MacBook Pro 14" (M3) | 27" external display | Keyboard, mouse, USB-C hub |
| Analyst / Consultant | MacBook Air 15" (M2) | 24" external display | Keyboard, mouse |
| Operations / Support | Lenovo ThinkPad L14 | 24" external display | Keyboard, mouse |
| Management | MacBook Pro 14" (M3) | 27" external display | Keyboard, mouse, USB-C hub |

Requests for non-standard equipment (e.g. a second monitor, a specific keyboard, a standing desk converter) must be submitted via the IT portal and approved by the employee's manager. Non-standard equipment requests are assessed on a case-by-case basis and subject to budget availability.

---

## 3. Software and System Access

### 3.1 Standard Access (Provisioned Automatically)
All employees receive the following on their first day:
- Company email (Microsoft 365)
- Slack workspace
- HR portal (leave, payroll, personal details)
- Internal intranet and document management system
- Video conferencing (Microsoft Teams)
- Password manager (1Password)

### 3.2 Role-Specific Access
Access to role-specific systems is provisioned based on the job role specified in the IT access request form. Common role-specific systems include:

- **Developers/Engineers:** GitHub Enterprise, Azure DevOps, watsonx.ai, watsonx.orchestrate, cloud platform consoles (AWS/Azure/IBM Cloud)
- **Analysts/Consultants:** Tableau, Power BI, watsonx.governance, data warehouse access
- **Finance:** Xero, expense management platform
- **Sales/Account Management:** Salesforce CRM

### 3.3 Requesting Additional Access
If an employee requires access to a system not covered by their standard role provisioning, they must:
1. Submit an access request via the IT portal
2. Provide a business justification
3. Obtain approval from their direct manager
4. For sensitive systems (financial data, customer data, production environments), additional sign-off from the IT Security team is required

Access requests are typically fulfilled within 2 working days for standard systems and up to 5 working days for systems requiring security review.

---

## 4. Acceptable Use

Company-issued equipment and systems are provided for business purposes. Incidental personal use is permitted provided it does not:
- Interfere with work responsibilities
- Consume excessive bandwidth or storage
- Involve accessing illegal content or pirated software
- Put company data or systems at risk

Employees must not:
- Install unlicensed or unapproved software on company devices
- Disable or tamper with security software (antivirus, endpoint management, MDM)
- Connect company devices to untrusted networks without using the company VPN
- Share login credentials with any other person, including colleagues

---

## 5. Remote Work and VPN

Employees working remotely must connect to the company VPN for any access to internal systems. The VPN client is pre-installed on all company devices.

When working from a public location (café, airport, hotel), employees must use the VPN at all times and avoid conducting sensitive conversations or displaying sensitive information in public view.

---

## 6. Data Handling and Security

Employees must store work files on company-approved cloud storage (SharePoint/OneDrive). Local storage of sensitive company or customer data is prohibited unless explicitly approved for offline work scenarios.

Sensitive data categories that require additional care:
- Customer personal information (names, contact details, account data)
- Financial records and contracts
- Employee records
- Intellectual property and proprietary product information

If an employee suspects a security incident (lost device, suspicious email, unauthorised access), they must report it to the IT Security team immediately at security@apexsolutions.internal. Do not attempt to investigate or resolve a suspected security incident independently.

---

## 7. Equipment Care and Loss

Employees are responsible for the reasonable care of company equipment. In the event of accidental damage, the employee must notify IT within 24 hours. Intentional damage or gross negligence may result in the employee being held liable for repair or replacement costs.

Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT Security and the employee's manager immediately. IT Security will remotely wipe the device as soon as the loss is reported.

---

## 8. Offboarding — Equipment Return and Access Revocation

Upon resignation or termination, employees must return all company equipment on or before their last working day. All system access will be revoked at end-of-day on the employee's last day.

Equipment must be returned in its original condition (reasonable wear and tear accepted) along with all accessories. Failure to return equipment may result in deductions from the final pay.

---

## 9. How to Submit an IT Request

All IT requests are managed through the IT portal accessible at `it.apexsolutions.internal`. The following request types are available:

- **New employee setup** — submitted by hiring manager before start date
- **Additional access request** — submitted by employee with manager approval
- **Equipment fault or replacement** — submitted by employee directly
- **Software installation request** — submitted by employee with manager approval
- **Security incident report** — submitted by employee, escalated immediately to IT Security

For urgent issues, contact the IT helpdesk directly: `helpdesk@apexsolutions.internal` or extension 1100.
